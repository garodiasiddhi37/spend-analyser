# Next Gen Spend Analyser

A free spend analysis tool for the procurement community, built by **Next Gen Procurement**.

Upload a spend or invoice export from Coupa, SAP Ariba, Jaggaer, Ivalua, your ERP or Excel and get:

- A spend cube you can slice by category, supplier, business unit, cost centre and month
- Tail spend and supplier concentration (Pareto)
- PO and contract compliance by business unit
- Possible duplicate invoices (exact and near)
- Price variance on the same item across suppliers
- A savings opportunity model with your own assumptions
- Supplier name clean-up and category mapping
- An Excel report of the full analysis

**Privacy:** everything is calculated in your browser. Your spend file is never uploaded or stored.

The sample data (Harbour & Hale Industries) is fictional.

Estimates only, not financial, legal or compliance advice.
